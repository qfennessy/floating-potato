    All 20 worlds

    Each world has its own launch point, its own weather (the height where north-south drift flips moves between 52 and 60 km), and its own random eruptions. A fixed balloon gets one altitude for every world, picked before launch.

    Bars under each score, left at the finish:envelope healthballast (climbing)gas (sinking). ✕ marks a lost balloon. Hover for details. Each world's winner is shown in its balloon's colour, underlined; ★ marks the 5 best runs overall. Click any score to watch that run.
